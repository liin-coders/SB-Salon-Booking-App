Privacy Policy.

SB Salon uses the information you provide when requesting an appointment, such as your name, contact details, selected services and preferred date/time, to manage and respond to your booking.

How information is used

Booking details are used to coordinate appointments and provide salon services. Website visit analytics may record basic visit timestamps. Do not submit information that is not needed for your appointment.

Storage and third-party services

Appointment and analytics data may be stored using Supabase. Availability and handling are subject to the services and security settings configured by the site operator.

Access and requests

For questions about your booking information or to request a correction, contact SB Salon directly using the contact details provided on the website.

Updates

This policy may be updated as the website's features change.
